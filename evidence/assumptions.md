# Synthetic assumptions

- No real company, transaction, employee, asset, contract or credential is represented.
- Costs and revenue-at-risk coefficients are illustrative.
- `evidence_ready` simulates accepted readiness evidence; no environment was queried.
- TSA forecasts are deterministic scenarios, not legal interpretations.
- Identity findings are synthetic and do not indicate a breach.
- The model demonstrates mechanics, not a completed integration or separation.
